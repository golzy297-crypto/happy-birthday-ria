# Since the last time… — Happy Birthday Ria

> *“Last year, I tried to put our story into a video.*  
> *Turns out, a lot can happen in a year.”*

A cinematic, personal birthday website celebrating **Ria** (turning 23) from **Dhruv**, documenting everything that happened over this past year, and offering quiet gratitude for the journey shared.

Rather than replaying Season 1 (the wallpaper, Insti, Falguni, etc.), this website acknowledges last year's video as the foundation and tells the story of **this specific year across 10 bespoke interactive chapters**.

---

## 🕊️ The 10 Bespoke Chapters

### 01 • First Paper Acceptance 📝
- **Treatment**: Academic manuscript card with acceptance badge.
- **Story**: Your first research paper acceptance. Ria was right there at your place when the email came through, turning an academic triumph into an unforgettable relationship memory.
- **Media**: `assets/images/paper-acceptance.jpg`

### 02 • Our One-Year Squash Arc 🏸
- **Treatment**: Mini-movie centerpiece with a match statistics scoreboard.
- **Scoreboard**:
  - Matches Played: 100+
  - Questionable Line Calls: Countless
  - Arguments Over Points: Way too many
  - Refused To Accept Score: Every weekend
  - Post-Match Smoothies / Food: 100% Attendance
- **Media**: `assets/videos/squash.mp4` (with `assets/images/squash-poster.jpg` thumbnail)

### 03 • ACL: The Comeback 📄 → 🏆
- **Treatment**: Bad reviewer scores displayed first (*“Yeah… they absolutely cooked us”*), followed by `⚡ REVISION MODE ACTIVATED ⚡`, late-night turnaround, and victory announcement (*“Bad reviews: 1. Us: 1.”*).
- **Media**: `assets/images/acl-acceptance.jpg`

### 04 • The Birthday Surprise 🎂
- **Treatment**: The symmetry of birthdays.
- **Story**: *“Last year you made my birthday feel special. This year, I wanted to make sure you knew I never forgot how that felt.”*
- **Media**: `assets/images/birthday-surprise.jpg`

### 05 • The Airport ✈️
- **Treatment**: Visually stark, quiet, spacious, and solemn.
- **Story**: The airport before leaving for the US. The turning point where life physically shared in the same city transitioned into holding on across distance.
- **Media**: `assets/images/airport.jpg`

### 06 • Long Distance 🌎
- **Treatment**: Long-distance dashboard with dual time-zone clocks (Mumbai IST ⇄ US) and FaceTime / screenshot cards.
- **Story**: Late night calls with mismatched hours, calculating time zones, sending random updates, and navigating life across oceans.
- **Media**: `assets/images/distance-1.jpg`, `assets/images/distance-2.jpg`

### 07 • The Odds: 97.3% 📊
- **Treatment**: Large bold **97.3%** empirical survival metric with breakdowns (squash dispute recovery: 99.1%, timezone calls answered: 98.5%, ACL stress survived: 100%).
- **Callout**: *“High variance in arguments, zero variance in caring.”*

### 08 • Our Petty Fights™ ⚡
- **Treatment**: Playful opening transitioning into sincere, grounded maturity.
- **Story**:
  *“And apparently, 8,000 miles of distance wasn't enough of a challenge, so we decided to fight about stupid things too.”*
  Leading sincerely into apologizing for calling things “petty” and acknowledging that her feelings—especially feeling unappreciated and unheard—were genuinely important.

### 09 • Things I Don't Say Enough About You 🌸
- **Treatment**: A complete departure from the relationship recap—dedicated 100% to celebrating **who Ria is as an individual**:
  - *You remember tiny details people mention in passing.*
  - *Your joy for other people's success.*
  - *Your emotional radar when someone is quietly overwhelmed.*
  - *Your quiet authenticity—never performing for anyone.*
  - *Your resilience under pressure.*

### 10 • Today — Her Birthday 🎂
- **Treatment**: The unwritten ending, heartfelt birthday blessing, and personal letter:
  > *Last year's video ended with our story.*  
  > *This one doesn't.*  
  > *Because I genuinely don't know what happens next.*  
  > *But today isn't about figuring that out.*  
  > *Today is about you.*  
- **Birthday Wish**:
  > **Happy Birthday, Ria.**  
  > *I hope this year is kind to you.*  
  > *I hope you do things that scare you, find things that excite you, laugh until your stomach hurts, and become even more of the person you're meant to be.*  
  > *And wherever I fit into that story, I'm just grateful I got to be in these chapters. ❤️*
- **Letter Sheet**: A deckle-edged sheet with a wax seal signed by **Dhruv**, ending with *“Start from Chapter 01 ↺”*.
